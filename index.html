Every Gharwaala kitchen is factory-pressed at our Okhla Phase III partner facility using calibrated PUR edge-banding, IS:710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) marine plywood in wet zones, and certified German hardware from Blum, Hettich, and Hafele.

Why Module-Wise BOQs Beat Per-Sq-Ft Quotes

Most local contractors quote an opaque "per square foot" rate that hides low-density commercial board and generic hinges. At Gharwaala, you receive an itemised Bill of Quantities (BOQ) listing every cabinet module, shutter finish, Hettich or Blum hardware code, and countertop slab thickness before production begins. Estimate your exact layout in 60 seconds with our Modular Kitchen Cost Calculator or preview finishes on your own kitchen photo using our AI Kitchen Studio.

Dedicated Site Teams Across All 5 NCR Hubs

Our senior kitchen architects conduct laser site measurements and bring physical laminate, acrylic, PU, and quartz swatch boxes directly to your home across South, West & Central Delhi, Gurugram (DLF, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road), Noida & Greater Noida, Faridabad, and Ghaziabad & Indirapuram.

Engineered for Heavy Indian Cooking & Monsoon Humidity

Unlike generic flat-pack cabinetry designed for dry European kitchens, Gharwaala builds specifically for Indian households—incorporating high-suction baffled chimneys (1200–1500 m³/hr), 2mm PUR moisture-sealed edges, 75kg-rated tandem drawers for heavy cast-iron cookware and atta storage, and 10-year structural warranties against delamination or borer damage.
