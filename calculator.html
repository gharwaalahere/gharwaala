How Modular Kitchen Cost Is Calculated in Delhi NCR (2026 Running-Foot & Module Guide)

When homeowners in Delhi NCR begin comparing modular kitchen quotes, they often encounter confusing "per square foot" numbers ranging anywhere from ₹1,200/sq.ft. to ₹3,800/sq.ft. However, professional factory-built modular kitchens are engineered and priced by individual cabinet modules and running feet (Rft)—not raw wall area. A simple shutter cabinet with a single shelf costs a fraction of a 3-drawer Hettich InnoTech or Blum Legrabox tandem unit occupying the exact same wall footprint.

4 Core Factors That Influence Your Final Kitchen Quote

1. Layout Geometry & Corner Units

Straight kitchens have zero blind corners, keeping hardware costs low. L-shape layouts introduce one blind corner (typically fitted with an ₹18,000–₹28,000 Magic Corner or S-Carousel), while U-shape kitchens include two corners plus 30% more base and overhead cabinetry.

2. Loft Cabinets (Ceiling-Height Storage)

Most Delhi NCR apartments have 9.5 ft to 10.5 ft ceilings. Adding a 2-foot loft row above the standard 2-foot overhead cabinets eliminates dust traps and adds 25% extra storage for seasonal cookware, typically adding ₹25,000 to ₹45,000 to the project.

3. Drawer-to-Shutter Ratio

Modern ergonomic kitchens replace deep lower shelves with full-extension tandem drawer banks. Each 3-drawer tandem module adds ₹9,500–₹16,000 in precision German runners compared to a basic hinged shutter cabinet.

4. Tall Pantry & Built-In Appliance Towers

A 6-layer soft-close pull-out pantry unit (150kg capacity) or a dedicated tall housing unit for a built-in microwave and oven typically adds ₹28,000–₹52,000 depending on internal hardware brand.

Why is IS:710 BWP Marine Plywood recommended over commercial MDF or particle board?

Indian kitchens experience heavy steam, oil tempering (tadka), and wet floor mopping. Particle board and MDF swell rapidly when exposed to moisture. IS:710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) marine plywood is bonded with undiluted phenol-formaldehyde resin that withstands 72 hours of boiling water without delamination.
