How We Engineer Every Kitchen

Engineering Standards, Factory Precision & Delhi NCR Studio Process

Every Indian kitchen endures intense heat, oil tempering (tadka), heavy wet grinding, and high monsoon humidity. Our Okhla Industrial Estate Phase III dispatch and manufacturing workflow is engineered specifically for these conditions.

1. Certified IS:710 BWP Marine Ply & HDHMR

We never use commercial particle board or MDF in wet zones. All sink, dishwasher, and hob base units use calibrated 18mm IS:710 Boiling Water Proof marine plywood bonded with waterproof phenolic resin, paired with high-density Action Tesa HDHMR in dry wall cabinets.

2. German PUR Edge-Banding & CNC Accuracy

Instead of manual carpenter pasting that leaves visible black glue lines, our shutters undergo automated Homag/Biesse CNC beam-saw cutting and polyurethane reactive (PUR) 2mm edge-banding that seals out steam and cooking oils permanently.

3. Blum, Hettich & Hafele Motion Hardware

Heavy Indian cookware and 25kg grain containers require high-load runners. We integrate 45kg–70kg Hettich InnoTech Atira and Blum Austria Legrabox tandem drawers, Aventos bi-fold overhead lift-ups, and anti-rust SS304 organizers tested for 1,00,000+ cycles.
