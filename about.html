About Gharwaala

We believe that a beautiful, functional kitchen shouldn't be a luxury reserved only for the wealthy.

Gharwaala was started with a simple mission: to connect homeowners in rising Indian cities directly with verified local professionals. This cuts out the expensive middlemen, confusing pricing, and unreliable workmanship.

Whether you have ₹50,000 or ₹5 Lakhs, our network ensures you get exactly the kitchen you want, built on time, and completely within your budget.

"Quality kitchens, honest pricing, local experts."
