What to Expect During Your Free At-Home Laser Measurement & 3D Consultation

Unlike traditional kitchen showrooms where you estimate dimensions on paper and receive a generic running-foot quote, Gharwaala sends a senior kitchen architect directly to your residence with digital Bosch laser measurement tools and physical swatch boxes. During this complimentary 60-minute home visit, we map your exact plumbing points, chimney ducting route, electrical load points, window sill heights, and beam depths to millimeter accuracy.

1. Physical Material & Hardware Audit

Inspect real cross-sections of IS:710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) marine plywood, Action Tesa HDHMR, 2mm anti-scratch European acrylic, 6-coat Matte PU lacquer, and KalingaStone/Quantra engineered quartz under your own kitchen lighting.

2. Live 3D Layout & Ergonomic Zoning

We configure your wet zone, prep counter, hob station, and tall appliance tower around Indian cooking workflows—integrating Hettich InnoTech or Blum Legrabox tandem drawers, magic corners, and concealed under-cabinet profile lighting.

3. Itemised Module-by-Module BOQ

Within 24 hours of your site measurement, you receive a transparent, zero-obligation Bill of Quantities (BOQ) detailing every cabinet carcass, shutter finish, hardware part number, and 45-day installation milestone.
