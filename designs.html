How to Choose the Right Modular Kitchen Layout & Shutter Finish for Indian Homes

Selecting a kitchen design goes far beyond color palettes. In Delhi NCR apartments and builder floors, your layout must balance the Golden Work Triangle (refrigerator, sink, and hob separated by 4 to 9 feet) with high-capacity storage for pressure cookers, masala pull-outs, heavy grain containers, and RO water purifiers.

For compact 2BHK and 3BHK apartments (65–95 sq.ft.), an L-Shaped or Parallel Galley layout prevents aisle congestion while accommodating a 3-drawer tandem unit beneath the hob and a dedicated wet sink zone in IS:710 BWP marine plywood. For larger 3BHK/4BHK residences and independent floors (105–150+ sq.ft.), a U-Shaped or Open Island kitchen allows you to separate wet washing from dry meal prep and integrate a tall built-in oven/microwave tower alongside a 6-shelf pull-out pantry.
