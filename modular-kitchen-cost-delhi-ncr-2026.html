Modular Kitchen Cost in Delhi NCR (2026): Complete Price Breakdown

What a modular kitchen really costs in Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad this year, and the line items that quietly push quotes up.

By Gharwaala Design Team | Published April 23, 2026 | Updated October 6, 2026 | 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • A small straight or L-shaped kitchen (50–70 sq ft) typically costs around ₹1.2L–₹2L in Delhi NCR.
  • A medium L-shaped kitchen (70–100 sq ft) generally falls between ₹2L and ₹4.5L.
  • Large U-shaped or parallel kitchens (100–150 sq ft) usually start around ₹4L and can exceed ₹7L.
  • Hardware and accessories can make up as much as 30% of the cabinetry budget.
  • Civil work, countertops and appliances are usually quoted separately. Always ask.

"What will my kitchen cost?" It's the first question almost every homeowner asks, and the one that gets the vaguest answers. Quotes for what looks like the same kitchen can differ by lakhs, because two contractors are rarely pricing the same materials, hardware or scope.

This guide sets out realistic 2026 price ranges for factory-finished modular kitchens in Delhi NCR, explains what drives the number up or down, and lists the extras that often appear only after work begins. All figures are indicative estimates for cabinetry and exclude civil work and appliances unless stated.

What drives the cost of a modular kitchen?

1. Size and layout

The running length of your base and wall cabinets is the biggest multiplier. A U-shape or island uses far more modules, corner solutions and countertop than a straight run of the same width.

2. Board material (the carcass)

Cabinets are built from MDF, HDHMR, commercial plywood or BWP (boiling water proof) plywood. BWP costs more per square foot but is the safest choice for wet zones like the sink base.

3. Shutter finish

Laminate is the most affordable and durable option. Acrylic gives a premium high-gloss finish at a higher price, while PU paint and natural veneer sit at the top of the range. See our kitchen laminates guide for a full comparison.

4. Hardware and accessories

Soft-close hinges, tandem drawers, corner carousels, lift-up mechanisms and tall pantry pull-outs from premium brands such as Hettich, Häfele or Blum can make up as much as 30% of the cabinetry budget. This is the line item where quotes vary the most.

Where to spend, and where to save

Spend on: BWP plywood for base cabinets (especially the sink unit), good drawer channels and hinges you use dozens of times a day, and a durable countertop.

Save on: HDHMR for overhead cabinets that rarely see water, high-gloss laminate instead of acrylic, and fewer specialised accessories. A well-planned drawer often beats an expensive corner gadget. Our guide to saving on custom cabinets goes deeper.

One of the smartest budget moves: a premium finish on the overheads where it's seen, and tough, budget-friendly matte laminate on the base units where it takes the knocks.

Hidden costs to watch for

These items are rarely included in a base modular quote. Ask about each one before you sign:

  • Civil work: breaking an old platform, shifting plumbing or electrical points, re-tiling or false ceiling.
  • Countertop and backsplash: granite, quartz and dado tiles are usually billed separately from the cabinets.
  • Appliances: hob, chimney, built-in oven, microwave and dishwasher. Finalise models before production so cut-outs fit exactly.
  • Sink and faucet: often excluded, or included only as a basic model.
  • Lighting: profile and under-cabinet lights are frequently an add-on.
  • Transport, installation and taxes: confirm whether GST and site delivery are included in the quoted figure.
Red flag: a single lump-sum figure with no brand names. If the quote doesn't name the board, laminate and hardware brands, you can't compare it with anything else, or hold anyone to it.

Does cost vary across Delhi NCR?

Material prices are broadly similar across the region. Differences come mostly from apartment type and site access: high-rise societies in Gurugram and Noida may have lift-booking rules and working-hour restrictions, while older builder floors in Delhi often need more civil correction for uneven walls and plumbing. How to compare quotes and choose a contractor

  1. Insist on a line-item quote listing board type and brand, laminate brand and thickness, and hardware brand per module.
  2. Compare like for like. A cheaper quote with unbranded channels isn't really cheaper.
  3. Ask for a 3D design before production so you can check drawer placement and appliance fit.
  4. Check the warranty separately for board, hardware and workmanship.
  5. Confirm the timeline. Factory-made modular kitchens typically take 3–5 weeks from design sign-off to installation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average modular kitchen cost in Delhi?

For a standard 2BHK kitchen in Delhi, the average cost of modular cabinetry ranges between ₹1.5 lakh and ₹3 lakh, depending mainly on board material, shutter finish and hardware. Civil work, countertop and appliances are usually extra.

How much does a modular kitchen cost per square foot in Delhi NCR?

Pricing is usually quoted per running foot or per module rather than per square foot of floor area, so per-square-foot figures can be misleading. As a guide, a 50–70 sq ft kitchen costs about ₹1.2L–₹2L and a 70–100 sq ft L-shaped kitchen about ₹2L–₹4.5L.

Which material is best for a modular kitchen in Delhi?

BWP (boiling water proof) plywood is recommended for base cabinets, especially under the sink, to cope with Indian cooking and cleaning habits. HDHMR is a cost-effective option for overhead cabinets.

How long does it take to install a modular kitchen in Delhi NCR?

A factory-finished modular kitchen typically takes 3 to 5 weeks from design approval to final installation, plus any time needed for civil work.

Are there hidden costs in a modular kitchen?

Often, yes. Civil work, countertops, backsplash tiles, sink and faucet, appliances, lighting, transport and GST may be excluded from the base quote. Ask for an itemised quotation that lists what's included.
