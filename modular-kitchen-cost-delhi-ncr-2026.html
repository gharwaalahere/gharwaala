1. What Affects the Cost of a Modular Kitchen?

The final quotation for a kitchen upgrade isn't just a random number. The kitchen renovation cost Delhi 2026 is driven by four primary factors:

  • Kitchen Size & Layout: A small straight kitchen requires far fewer modules than a large U-shaped layout with an island.
  • Core Materials: The "carcass" (the box of the cabinet) can be made of MDF, HDHMR, Commercial Plywood, or BWP (Boiling Water Proof) Plywood. BWP is essential for wet areas but costs more.
  • Exterior Finishes: Laminate is budget-friendly and highly durable. Acrylic offers a premium high-gloss mirror finish but costs significantly more. PU Paint and Veneers sit at the highest end of the spectrum.
  • Hardware & Accessories: Soft-close hinges, heavy-duty tandem drawers, magic corners, and lift-up systems from brands like Hettich or Blum can easily account for 30% of the total budget.

3. Material Choices: Where to Spend and Where to Save

To optimize your budget, you must be strategic about materials.

The Carcass (Inner Cabinets)

Do not compromise on the base cabinets, especially under the sink. Always use BWP (Boiling Water Proof) plywood here to prevent water damage. For overhead wall cabinets, which are rarely exposed to water, you can safely save money by opting for high-quality HDHMR (High-Density High Moisture Resistance) boards.

The Shutters (Outer Finish)

While Acrylic looks stunning, it requires high maintenance and is prone to scratches. High-gloss laminates offer a very similar aesthetic at nearly half the price. Textured laminates are also excellent for hiding fingerprints and daily wear-and-tear.

4. Hidden Costs to Watch Out For

When comparing quotes from local carpenters or boutique studios, watch out for these easily hidden fees:

  • Civil Work: Tearing down old platforms, changing plumbing lines, or re-tiling is rarely included in the base modular quote.
  • Countertops: Quartz or Granite countertops are almost always billed separately from the cabinetry.
  • Appliances: Built-in hobs, chimneys, microwaves, and dishwashers are external costs. Ensure your designer accounts for their exact dimensions before manufacturing begins.
